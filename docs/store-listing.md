# Google Play store listing

Copy and assets for Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing.
Every claim below matches the shipped app. Keep it that way when editing:
Play rejects misleading listings, and false claims cost ratings.

Play has no keyword field. It ranks on the **title**, **short description** and
**full description**, with the title weighted most. Repeat each target keyword
2–4 times in the full description, written naturally; more than that reads as
stuffing, which Play penalises.

| Priority | Keywords                                                                   |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Primary  | mantra jaap counter, japa mala, mala counter, naam jap, jaap counter       |
| Category | 108 beads, digital mala, chanting app, tally counter                       |

Every screen shown is a real capture of the app. The music is the app's own
tanpura loop, and the bell is the app's own round chime, so the video is safe
to monetise-check and needs no licences.

Upload it to YouTube, then paste the link in Play Console → Main store
listing → Video:

- Visibility **Public** or **Unlisted** (not Private). Turn **off** ads on the
  video and leave it **not** age-restricted, or Play won't show it.
- Use the same video for en-IN and hi-IN. Its on-screen text is English with
  Hindi accents, which reads naturally to the bilingual audience.
- With a video, Play puts the feature graphic behind a centred play button.
  Check the listing preview, and if the button covers "Your mantra jaap
  counter", move the text in the feature graphic further left.
- The first 3–5 s matter most: most viewers stop by then. If installs from the
  video page stay flat, test a hook that opens directly on the counter.

## Iterating

- Use Play Console → Store listing experiments to change one thing at a time:
  the icon, the first screenshot, or the short description.
- Before festivals (Maha Shivratri, Navratri, Shravan, Janmashtami,
  Hanuman Jayanti), add one line near the top of the description naming the
  festival and its mantra, and remove it afterwards.
- After 4–6 weeks, check Play Console → Store performance → search terms,
  and swap out keywords that bring no installs.
